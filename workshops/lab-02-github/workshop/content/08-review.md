---
title: "Step 6: review your partner's pull request"
---

Swap pull request links with your partner, and open theirs. Review it as the CAB would:

1. *Files changed*: read the diff.
2. Hover a changed line and click the blue **+** that appears. Comment: a question, a suggestion. *Start a
   review*.
3. *Review changes*, at the top right: *Approve*, then *Submit review*.

The approval and the discussion stay with the change, for anyone who later asks why it was made.

GitHub doesn't let you approve your own pull request. Working alone? Comment on a line of your own diff
instead: *Add single comment*.

```examiner:execute-test
prefix: Check
description: Click when your pull request has its review
name: reviewed
title: Your pull request is reviewed
```
